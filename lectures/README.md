# Ontology lectures

A ten-part audio course built from this repository's notes, plus a research
pass that filled the gaps the original harvest did not cover.

| Path | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `COURSE-PLAN.md` | Syllabus, per-lecture plan, and the source notes each lecture draws on |
| `transcripts/` | Ten plain-text transcripts, numbered in listening order |
| `VOICE.md` | ElevenReader setup and the Michael Caine voice |

## The ten lectures

1. Three Things People Mean by "Ontology"
2. From Aristotle to Wolff: Where the Word Came From
3. Quine, and Ontology as a Property of Theories
4. The Cathedral: Cyc, and the Birth of the Engineering Sense
5. The Semantic Web: A Vision and the Stack It Built
6. How the Machinery Actually Works
7. Building One for Real
8. The Criticisms That Landed
9. Palantir: The Ontology as an Operational Layer
10. The Politics of an Installed Ontology, and What Comes Next

About 29,000 words, roughly three hours and twenty minutes.

## What the research pass added

The original harvest covered standards, tooling, reasoners, and vocabularies
well. It had nothing on the history of the idea, the criticism of it, or
Palantir. Thirteen notes were added to fill those gaps, and they are indexed
and searchable through the `ontology-chat` skill like every other note:

- History: `ontology-word-history`, `quine-ontological-commitment`,
  `gruber-ontology-definition`, `cyc-lenat-1995`,
  `semantic-web-2001-vision`, `semantic-web-retrospective`,
  `knowledge-graph-turn`
- Criticism: `doctorow-metacrap`, `shirky-ontology-is-overrated`,
  `bowker-star-sorting-things-out`, `palantir-ontology-critique`
- Operational: `palantir-ontology`
- Present: `llm-ontology-debate`

`SYNTHESIS.md` gained four sections covering the same ground across sources.

Raw captures from the research pass live in `research/` and are not tracked,
matching how `sources/` is handled.
