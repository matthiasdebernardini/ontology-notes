# History of the Word "Ontology"

**What it is**
The philosophical lineage behind the word that computer science borrowed. Aristotle named the subject without naming the word: in Book IV of the *Metaphysics* he calls it "first philosophy," the study of being qua being — the attributes a thing has because of its very nature rather than because of what kind of thing it happens to be. The Latin coinage came almost two thousand years later. Jacob Lorhard used *ontologia* in *Ogdoas Scholastica* in 1606. Rudolf Goclenius printed it in his *Lexicon philosophicum* in 1613. Johann Clauberg is often credited but came later. Christian Wolff put it into general circulation with *Philosophia prima sive ontologia* in 1730, defining it as *scientia entis in genere, quatenus ens est* — the science of being in general, insofar as it is being.

**Key concepts**
Being qua being; first philosophy; general metaphysics versus special metaphysics; *ontologia* 1606; Wolff's demonstrative method; Kant's attack on general metaphysics; Husserl's "formal ontology"; Heidegger's *Dasein*; the split between ontology as a question about reality and ontology as an artifact you can build.

**How you'd use it**
Use this history to keep three senses of the word apart when reading anything about ontologies. Sense one is the philosophical discipline: what exists. Sense two is a specific philosophical theory of what exists, which is why philosophers say "Quine's ontology" the way engineers say "the FIBO ontology." Sense three is the computer-science artifact: a file containing a formal vocabulary. Wolff's contrast between general metaphysics (being as such) and special metaphysics (souls, world, God) is the direct ancestor of the split between upper ontologies such as BFO and domain ontologies such as GO.

**Pitfalls & lessons**
Do not treat the engineering sense as a straightforward continuation of the philosophical one. Wolff's ontology aimed to be the single true account of being, derived deductively. An engineering ontology is chosen for a purpose and is one of many possible models of the same domain. Papers and vendors slide between the two senses, and the slide usually smuggles in a claim of objectivity the artifact has not earned.
